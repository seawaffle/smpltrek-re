128 × 128 display, 16 gray levels, 48 kHz audio. Unmodified stock firmware on the compiled core; inputs are switch, encoder and panel-UART signals only.

Button positions follow the stock test page's switch map and the LED table in analysis/led_table.py. Not checked against a device: which pad is which analogue channel (assumed to follow the pad LED numbering) and the left/right audio routing. The two input-gain knobs and the microphone gain are drawn but not emulated.
